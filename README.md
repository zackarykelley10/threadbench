# Threadbench

A free embroidery workspace that runs in your browser, built for the Brother PE770 and its 4 × 4, 5 × 7 and 5 × 12 repositionable hoops.

Open it at **https://zackarykelley10.github.io/threadbench/**. It works on computers and phones, needs no install, and your designs never leave your device.

## What it does

- Lettering with built-in fonts or your own font files, curved text and monograms
- Applique shapes, letters and artwork, with cutting templates
- SVG artwork to stitches; open and combine PES and DST designs
- Solid, cross-stitch, motif and embossed fills
- Thread charts for 10 brands, thread lists and printable worksheets
- Stitch-out simulator
- Split large designs across several hoopings, or across the positions of the 5 × 12 repositionable hoop
- Saves PES, DST, EXP and JEF
